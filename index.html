关于我

I'm OLIVER.Z，a freelance photographer based in Hong Kong at present. Virgo. Holding a Ph.D. degree.

I love the portrait of natural light, as I can feel your innocent and pure emotions from those fascinating moments. I try to understand, record, and characterize your emotions in association with surroundings, as well as to freeze the passing youth for you.

As for me, photography is not only to present beautiful images and retouching techniques, but also to convey a thought and mood. I'm used to interpreting the world through my lens, craving for passion and beauty. It's also a reflection of a fragile and romantic side of me, in the belief that all things are bright and beautiful. And if you listen carefully, my silent but thoughtful pictures will tell a story.
